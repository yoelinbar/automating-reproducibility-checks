# Data provenance

## `miske/`

The files under `miske/` are redistributed verbatim from the public data
deposit accompanying:

> Miske, O., Abatayo, A. L., et al. (2026). Investigating the reproducibility
> of the social and behavioural sciences. *Nature*, 652.

which is an output of the SCORE project (Systematizing Confidence in Open
Research and Evidence):

> Alipourfard, N., et al. (2021). Systematizing Confidence in Open Research and
> Evidence (SCORE). https://doi.org/10.31235/osf.io/46mnb

Files included (original SCORE/Miske directory layout preserved):

| path | rows | role in this analysis |
|---|---|---|
| `analysis_ready/paper_metadata.csv` | 4,000 | paper `type` and `is_covid` flags |
| `analysis_ready/pr_outcomes.csv` | 700 | per-paper `data_available` flag |
| `analysis_ready/repro_outcomes.csv` | 651 | per-claim Miske verdicts + `repro_version_of_record` |
| `raw_data/repro_vor.csv` | 654 | per-report canonical-row flag |

These files are unmodified copies of the deposited originals as they were on May 1, 2026. Only the four
files consumed by `build_disagreements.R` are bundled here; the full SCORE
deposit contains substantially more.

## `llm_analyst/`

`summary.csv` is the per-claim output the LLM analyst (Claude Opus
4.7) produced for the accompanying manuscript. It is original to this
work.

`reports/` contains the LLM analyst's full per-paper reproduction report
(`<paper_id>_<author>_<year>.md`, one per audited paper, 73 in total). These
are verbatim copies of the analyst's working write-ups and are original to
this work.

## `adjudicated_disagreements.csv`

The human adjudication of the 47 LLM-vs-Miske disagreements (the `Errors in`
column: SCORE / Neither / LLM / Both, plus supporting notes). This was coded
by the authors, not generated by an LLM. It is original to this work.
